Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you.

Forge was built around one simple principle: your workout data belongs on your device, not on our servers.

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Forge is an offline-first interval training application. We do not require user accounts, cloud synchronization, or personal profiles to use the app.

Most information created while using Forge remains stored locally on your device.

Information We Collect

Forge does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Health records
  • Payment information

Your Workout Data

Workout history, custom workouts, statistics, mastery progress, settings and habits are stored locally on your device.

Forge does not upload this information to our servers.

Backup & Export

Forge allows you to export your own backups. Backups are created only when you choose to create them. You decide where they are stored and how they are shared.

Internet Connection

Forge is designed to work completely offline. An internet connection is only required for downloading the app, future updates, or optional external links.

Third Party Services

Forge does not use advertising networks. Forge does not use analytics services. Forge does not sell personal data.

Children's Privacy

Forge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when new features are introduced. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
