FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Forge.

Does Forge work offline?

Yes. Forge is designed to work completely offline after installation. Your workouts, statistics, progress, and settings remain available without an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

No. Forge never requires an account or registration. Simply install the app and start training.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device. Forge does not upload your workout history, settings, or personal progress to the cloud.

Can I create my own workouts and training plans?

Absolutely. Forge lets you create everything from simple interval timers to advanced multi-phase training plans. Save your workouts, organize them, and adapt them as your training evolves.

Does Forge include workout presets?

Yes. Forge comes with ready-to-use workout presets and also lets you build, duplicate, and customize your own routines.

Can I use Forge for more than HIIT?

Absolutely. Forge supports strength training, calisthenics, cardio, mobility, stretching, EMOM, Tabata, circuits, recovery sessions, and fully custom interval workouts. Whether you're training at home or in the gym, Forge adapts to your routine.

How does Forge track my progress?

Forge helps you understand your training through workout history, consistency tracking, weekly volume, Mastery Tracks, and long-term insights that grow with every session.

What is Forge Score?

Forge Score is a long-term consistency score that combines multiple aspects of your training into a single, easy-to-understand metric. Instead of counting workouts alone, it rewards balanced and sustainable progress over time.

What is Smart Coaching?

Smart Coaching turns your training history into useful guidance with daily briefings, workout recommendations, recovery insights, and personalized feedback that helps you make better training decisions.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Forge supports manual backup and restore, allowing you to export, transfer, and restore your data whenever you choose.

Will my data be safe if I switch devices?

Yes. You can create manual backups of your workouts, progress, and settings and restore them on another device whenever you need to.

Does Forge contain ads or tracking?

No. Forge contains no advertisements, no user tracking, and no analytics designed to monitor your personal training habits.

Which devices are supported?

Forge is currently available for Android devices.
