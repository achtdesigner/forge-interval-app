The experience

A workout experience designed to disappear.

When your workout begins, everything else fades away. Large timers, clear phase colors, ambient music, and distraction free controls keep your attention where it belongs: on your training.

  •  

    Fullscreen Focus

    A clean fullscreen interface with large timers and clear visual cues keeps your attention on every interval.

  •  

    Ambient Music

    Stay in your rhythm with built in ambient sound packs designed for calm focus, energy, or recovery.

  •  

    Touch Lock

    Lock the screen during intense sessions to prevent accidental taps while you move.

  •  

    Audio & Haptics

    Voice cues, countdown sounds, and optional haptic feedback help you stay in sync without constantly watching the timer.
